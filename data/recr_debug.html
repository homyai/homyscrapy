Investing in Costa Rica Real Estate: Best Locations for Your Next Property Investment

Costa Rica has an amazing tropical climate and beautiful weather all year round. The laid-back lifestyle of the nation gives the feeling of "island living," and the country is becoming a hot spot for many ex-pats that are looking to have a second home. When looking for a new home away from home, it's a good idea to understand the communities that you are looking at, and to enlist the help of a local real estate agency. They can help you understand the local culture, the atmosphere, and the offerings in each different area. It's also important to understand the properties you're looking into and how they will best suit your lifestyle. Here are the top five places to look for real estate in Costa Rica.

Tropical Beaches of Costa Rica

Playa Brasilito

Playa Brasilito offers a perfect mix of nightlife, tours, and laid-back culture. The town is known for high-quality seafood, brought in on boats by the local fishermen and served in many of the local restaurants. The town square is filled with bars, restaurants of many varieties, street food, and tour groups run by the large local population. 

The beautiful beaches in the area are a great way to spend the day relaxing or horse riding. 

Playa Brasilito has many investment options available to those looking for a second home in the area, from plots of land to homes located near the beach. 

Playa Conchal

Playa Conchal is well-known for its snorkeling and swimming beaches. The crescent-shaped beach has sparkling blue water and very little swell and wave activity.

If you are looking at getting into a place that is in the "up-and-coming" arena, this town may be for you. There are a lot of opportunities for day adventures when you feel like exploring, or you can just sit back and enjoy the view overlooking the ocean. 

Playa Conchal is still developing and growing. There are high-end real estate options available and quaint homes that would make a perfect second house for your family. 

Playa Grande

If you're looking to live an active outdoor life, Playa Grande may be just the place for you. One of the most beautiful beach towns in the country, Playa Grande is known as a nesting site for leatherback turtles, for its gorgeous blue waters, and its excellent surfing. Marino las Baulas National Park is nearby to protect the turtles and other wildlife.

The town has real estate investment opportunities for just about everyone. Many residences have a view of the ocean, and there are plots available on the beach as well. 

Playa Langosta

Playa Langosta is a high-end paradise and more developed than some of the other locations. The neighborhoods are well-planned, and the beachfront is dotted with beautiful homes. This town has fewer people and less traffic than some of the other towns and is a little more tranquil. There are restaurants and a small marketplace that cater to the locals and offer excellent service to the residents. 

The high-end community has properties available for both homeowners and investors, and properties here will have a higher return on investment than in some other locations.

Photo from WikiCommons

Playa Tamarindo

Playa Tamarindo is well-known for its serene white sand beaches and the movie Endless Summer II, which was filmed in this beautiful beach town. The town has amazing nightlife opportunities, with both restaurants and bars for your pleasure. There are day excursions available when you are looking to get away, and opportunities to surf in the small waves, perfect for beginners. 

Properties are abundant here if you know where to look. You can find oceanview condos and homes ready to buy. 

Choosing Your Spot

Choosing the right spot to live or have a second home is never easy. Each town has its own unique properties and lifestyles. The right one is the one that best suits you and your family. If you're looking for a real estate property, work with a local agent. They can help you find your ideal place in this beautiful country.
